CALA VERDE — an open-world crime game in a golden-hour coastal city: 8 story jobs, a 5-star
CVPD dispatch with roadblocks and the CONDOR-2 helicopter, car theft and arcade driving,
on-foot combat, a two-person crew, a garage with buyable cars, saves, and 3 synth radio
stations. Built with three.js, fully offline, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## How to play

You are Leo Marín, a wheelman new to Cala Verde. Rico (driver) and Mara (hacker) are your
crew. Work through 8 story jobs — from a first meeting at the marina to the final Verde
bank job at 5 wanted stars — earn cash, buy faster cars in the garage, and stay ahead of
the CVPD.

- **WASD / arrows** — walk or drive (W accelerate, S brake/reverse, A/D steer)
- **Mouse drag** — orbit camera · **wheel** — zoom
- **E** — enter / exit a car (steal parked and traffic cars)
- **F** — fire weapon on foot · **1 / 2 / 3** — pistol / SMG / shotgun
- **Space** — handbrake in a car · **R** — cycle radio · **M** — mute
- **P / Esc** — pause · **Q** — crew ability (Mara scrambles one roadblock)

## Story jobs

1. First Light — meet Rico at the marina
2. Hot Wheels — steal the Comet GT, deliver it to the garage
3. The Getaway — pick up Mara, lose a 2-star tail
4. Bay Run — street race, 5 checkpoints
5. Silent Harbor — clear 3 dock guards on foot
6. Golden Hour — cross-town delivery against the clock
7. Roadblock Run — smash through roadblocks at 4 stars
8. The Verde Job — the bank, 5 stars, CONDOR-2 in the air, escape by boat

Side work never stops: street races pay, and the garage always has a
faster car for sale. Progress, cash, cars and crew save automatically.
